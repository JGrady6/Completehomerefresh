Questions About Interior & Exterior Painting

What does painting below the dirt line mean?

On exterior jobs I prep and paint the lowest few inches of siding, the part closest to the ground. In Florida's humid climate, moisture and rot tend to start there, and most painters skip it. Sealing that area helps protect the house from water coming in from the bottom up.

Do you help pick paint colors?

Yes. Color consultation is part of interior painting, so the finished room feels the way you want it to.

Can I pay after my house sells?

Deferred payment is available on my pre-listing package, which combines painting with other prep. With a signed contract, it can be paid at closing from the sale proceeds. If the home has not sold within 90 days of listing, the payment plan agreed in your estimate begins.

Do you use subcontractors?

No. I do the painting myself, so the person who gives you the estimate is the person in your home.

Which areas do you serve?

I travel to you across Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco Counties, including Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Seminole, and Palm Harbor.
