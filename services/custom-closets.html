Questions About Custom Closets

How does a custom closet project start?

With a space assessment and a conversation about how you actually use the room. From there I design a system around your belongings, using hanging zones, shelving, drawers, and specialty storage for shoes, accessories, and seasonal items.

Do you only do bedroom closets?

No. I also design pantry systems and built-in storage for home offices.

Should I add a custom closet before I list my home?

Closets are usually a buyer's upgrade, so I do not include them in my pre-listing packages. They make the most sense once you have bought a home, or when you plan to stay and enjoy them.

Who installs the system?

I install each system myself rather than handing it to a crew.
