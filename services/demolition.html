Before any renovation can begin, the old has to come out — and that work matters more than most homeowners realize. Sloppy demolition can damage subfloors, plumbing, and structural elements, turning a manageable renovation into a costly repair job before a single new tile is laid. Complete Home Refresh handles demolition carefully and cleanly, protecting what needs to be protected while clearing out everything that needs to go. Homeowners planning kitchen or bathroom renovations across Tampa Bay rely on this service as the critical first phase of their remodel.

Services include full kitchen cabinet demolition, bathroom tile removal from floors and walls, flooring removal across all types — ceramic, porcelain, vinyl, and hardwood. Working across Tampa Bay, Nachelle has handled tile removal jobs in homes of all sizes, from compact condos to sprawling single-family properties, always leaving the space clean and contractor-ready.

If you're planning a renovation and looking for tile removal near me in Tampa Bay, having this phase done right — with clean debris removal — will save you time and money at every subsequent stage of the project. It's unglamorous work, but it's the foundation everything else is built on.
