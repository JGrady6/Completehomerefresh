Clearing out a loved one's home — or managing a property that has accumulated decades of belongings — is one of the most emotionally and physically overwhelming tasks a family can face. Complete Home Refresh approaches every estate clean-out with genuine compassion, discretion, and efficiency that takes the burden off your shoulders completely. Families across Pinellas County — from Largo and Seminole to Treasure Island and Madeira Beach — have trusted Nachelle to handle this sensitive work with the care and respect it deserves.

The process covers everything: sorting and cataloging items of potential value, coordinating donations to local organizations, removing everything that can't be donated (Nachelle does the junk removal herself), and leaving the property completely clear and ready for its next chapter — whether that's a sale, a rental, or a renovation. Searching for an estate cleanout near me in the Tampa Bay area? Complete Home Refresh handles every aspect of the job so that you don't have to make countless phone calls, manage multiple vendors, or spend weekends you don't have sorting through a lifetime of belongings.

Questions About Estate Clean-Outs

What does an estate clean-out cover?

Sorting and cataloging items of potential value, coordinating donations to local organizations, removing everything that cannot be donated, and leaving the property clear for its next step.

Who handles the junk removal?

I do. I haul away what cannot be donated myself rather than arranging an outside company.

Do you handle tenant clean-outs too?

Yes. Clean-outs between occupancies are part of the work I do for property managers.

Is a clean-out part of the pre-listing package?

It can be, when the home needs it. It is a separate service from the rest of pre-listing prep, so it is only included when it is needed. See the Complete Pre-Listing Package for everything that can go with it.

How do I get an estimate?

On-site estimates are free. Call 727-599-5419 or use the contact page.
